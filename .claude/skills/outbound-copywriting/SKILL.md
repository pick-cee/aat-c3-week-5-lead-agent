# Outbound copywriting

Create three short cold emails and one short LinkedIn message for human review.
Never send anything. Each draft includes a subject when appropriate, body,
personalisation note, and the excerpt labels supporting every company-specific
line.

Email 1 opens with a relevant evidenced observation, connects it to the offer,
and asks a low-pressure question. Email 2 adds a different evidenced operational
angle. Email 3 is a brief final follow-up that makes it easy to say the timing or
fit is wrong. The LinkedIn message is concise and calm.

Good personalisation uses positioning, category, audience, hiring or scaling
signals, and public workflow clues. Weak personalisation includes "Loved what
you are building", "Your company looks impressive", and "I saw your website".

Write like a person, not a promotion. Avoid fake urgency, exaggerated claims,
generic praise, personal contact data, and em dashes. Do not invent facts. The
application enforces citation integrity, length, banned register, and contact
data checks even if this skill is not invoked.
